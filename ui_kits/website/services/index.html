Answers

The Services, Answered.

Can GHXSTSHIP act as the lead producer for an experiential or entertainment project?
Yes. GHXSTSHIP runs as the lead producer and accountable team for experiential design and production, owning scope, schedule, budget, crew, vendors, and technology across all nine phases, from Discover to Close, the way a lead producer owns a show.
Do you run venues and sites?
Yes. Venue & Site Operations is its own service: logistics, workforce and crew management, health, safety and compliance, and the day-of on the ground, plus programming, bookings, facility and vendor operations, and on-site teams for clubs, stadiums, arenas, and hospitality.
Do you manage tours and talent?
Yes. Tour & Talent Management is its own service: tour routing and advancing, artist and talent relations and logistics, travel and accommodation, crew rostering on the road, per-city venue advance, settlement and reporting, and the run of show, city after city.
Do you implement technology and systems?
Yes. Technology & Systems Implementation covers our four in-house platforms, the ATLVS operator console, COMPVSS for site and venue operations, the GVTEWAY public interface and marketplace, and the LEG3ND knowledge layer, some live today and some in build, plus the show systems we specify and install, third-party integrations, on-site installs, data, analytics, and the implementation and training to make it stick.
Which industries and verticals do you serve?
Festivals, concerts and tours, brand activations, immersive experiences, cruise ships, amusement parks, sporting events, TV, film and broadcast, premium hospitality, luxury retail, health and wellness, auto and motorsports, corporate and private, and philanthropy, for brands, promoters, venue operators, general contractors, and production directors worldwide.
