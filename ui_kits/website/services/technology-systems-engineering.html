Answers

Technology & Systems Engineering FAQ.

What are ATLVS, COMPVSS, GVTEWAY, and LEG3ND?
Four platforms that make up the technology layer of XPMS 2.6. ATLVS is the operator console: ERP, CRM, and project management on one record, from estimate to settlement. COMPVSS runs site and venue operations for deskless crews: rosters, scheduling, advancing, credentials, and day-of coordination. GVTEWAY is the public interface and marketplace: gigs, open calls, talent EPKs and crew profiles, vendor directories, and published RFQs. LEG3ND is the knowledge layer: the XPMS standard, courses and certifications, the priced catalog, the signage library, and the compliance engine. COMPVSS and LEG3ND are live today, the GVTEWAY marketplace is open with ticketing and stakeholder portals in build, and ATLVS is in build.
Is show technology part of the platforms?
No. Projection, playback, LED, show control, and content pipelines are GHXSTSHIP service capabilities. We specify, install, commission, and operate them under this vertical. They are not features of any of the four platforms.
Do you integrate with our existing stack?
Yes. We meet your stack where it is. We connect to the ticketing, finance, scheduling, access, and show-control systems you already run, build the data pipeline between them, and put the reporting on one set of dashboards so the numbers reconcile instead of arguing with each other.
Do you handle implementation and training?
Yes. That is the service: we configure the platforms for your operation, install and commission the show systems on site, migrate the data, and train your team, so the tools are running your shows, not sitting in a browser tab.
What is XPMS?
XPMS 2.6 is our Experiential Production Management Standard: nine gated production phases: Discover, Design, Advance, Procure, Build, Install, Operate, Amplify, and Close, mapped across ten department classes, 90 coordinates in the ATLVS Coordinate Matrix, so every deliverable maps to a class and a phase. The four platforms are the technology layer of XPMS 2.6, and technology is where every other discipline reports in.
Can we use the platforms without the full production service?
Yes. The platforms stand on their own. Run what is shipping today, COMPVSS and LEG3ND, with the GVTEWAY marketplace open and ATLVS in early access to production partners, and bring us in for as much or as little of the build and operate work as you want. Start at the platforms page.
