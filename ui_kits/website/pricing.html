Straight Answers

Engagement FAQ.

How do you price an engagement?
Every engagement is priced as a scoped proposal. We hear what you're building, define scope and deliverables against the nine-phase XPMS 2.6 lifecycle, and return a written proposal with a fee structure that fits the shape of the work. No rate card on a website can honestly price a festival and a single-room activation the same way, so we don't pretend one can.
Is there a minimum engagement?
No hard minimum. The practical floor is a defined scope we can be accountable for — a phase, a discipline, or a defined consulting block. If the honest answer is that you don't need us yet, we'll say so and point you at what you do need.
Do you travel?
Yes. We're headquartered in Miami with regional offices in Los Angeles, Chicago, and New York City, and satellite offices in Nashville, Denver, Las Vegas, and Phoenix, and we take work wherever the project is. Travel and per diem are line items in the proposal, not surprises on the invoice.
Are you insured and licensed?
We carry the insurance the work requires and provide certificates during contracting. Permits, licensing, and compliance specific to your venue and market are handled as part of the advance, phase three of the lifecycle, before anything loads in.
How fast can you mobilize?
It depends on the scope. An embedded specialist or a single-phase engagement can move quickly once the proposal is signed. A full production engagement follows the nine-phase lifecycle from Discover, and the calendar is set by your date, not ours. Either way you'll have a named lead and a schedule in the first conversation after signing.
Do you work with our existing vendors?
Yes. As the lead producer we manage the vendors you already trust alongside the ones we bring, under one scope and one schedule. We don't require you to drop relationships that work — we require everyone to hit their dates.
