Answers

Touring FAQ.

How is GHXSTSHIP different from a traditional agency?
Three ways: we own the full nine-phase XPMS 2.5 production lifecycle end to end, not just creative. We build and implement our own technology, ATLVS, COMPVSS, GVTEWAY, and LEG3ND. And operations is a dedicated vertical, Venue & Site Operations, with logistics, crew, compliance, and analytics run by its own team.
What does GHXSTSHIP do for tours?
End-to-end concert and tour production: show design and staging, technical production, routing, advancing, trucking, backline, and tour management — run through our Tour & Talent Management vertical on our nine-phase XPMS 2.5 lifecycle.
Who is tour production for?
Artists and management, tour managers, promoters, and labels who need one accountable production partner across every city.
How many cities can you route?
From single-market runs to multi-continent routings, with full logistics, advancing, and settlements.
