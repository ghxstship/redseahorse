## The four platforms (names fixed; never rename)

| Platform | What it actually is | Status today |
|---|---|---|
| **ATLVS** | The operator console for experiential productions: **ERP × CRM × PM** on one record store. Sales and CRM plus project, program, venue, design, estimating, governance, production, finance, procurement, and asset and logistics management. Runs on the **Sell to Settle spine**; the **Coordinate Matrix** is its task lens. | **In build.** Early access to production partners. |
| **COMPVSS** | **Site and venue operations** for deskless crews. Field and venue workforce ops: rosters, scheduling, advancing, credentials, day-of coordination, timesheets. An installable web app that runs full screen from the home screen. | **Live today.** |
| **GVTEWAY** | The **public interface and marketplace**: gigs, open calls, talent EPKs, crew profiles, vendor directories, published RFQs, stores, plus the host and commerce console. Ticketing and stakeholder portals are still being built. | **Marketplace open today**; ticketing + portals in build. |
| **LEG3ND** | The **knowledge layer** — organization hub, knowledge, and academy: the XPMS standard, courses and LMS, certifications, resources hub, the priced **catalog** of atoms/URIDs, the **signage library**, and the **compliance engine**, plus safety. | **Live today.** Catalog + credential verification public; the rest opens with your organization. |

## Hard rules

1. **LEG3ND is NOT a show/media/broadcast/playback product.** Never say it
   carries content, runs show control, does broadcast, or owns the Amplify
   phase. Any such claim is a factual error. It is the knowledge/standard/
   academy layer.
2. **State availability honestly** wherever the platforms are presented as a
   set. Never claim all four run every engagement today. Acceptable framing:
   "COMPVSS and LEG3ND are live today; GVTEWAY's marketplace is open with
   ticketing and portals in build; ATLVS is in build."
3. **ATLVS is more than project management** — say ERP, CRM, and project
   management, or "the operator console," not "PM software."
4. **GVTEWAY is more than the gate** — lead with the marketplace and public
   interface; ticketing/portals are forthcoming, not current capability.
5. **COMPVSS is site and venue operations**, not only "crew."
6. Show technology, projection, playback, LED, and content pipelines are
   **GHXSTSHIP service capabilities** delivered under Technology & Systems
   Implementation. They are NOT a platform product — describe them as show
   technology we implement, without attributing them to LEG3ND.
7. Do not name the engineering repo nickname anywhere in copy.
8. Keep the four platform links: https://atlvs.pro · /compvss · /gvteway ·
   /legend. Do not add extension products (CVRGO/OPVS/GVLLEY/Vault).
9. XPMS 2.5 (nine phases) remains our standard — unchanged by this pass.
10. Trademark line in terms.html is unchanged — do not edit it.
