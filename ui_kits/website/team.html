The Team

The Team That Runs the Build.

Every build is led by a senior team that stays with you from the first consultation to strike. Ranks map to roles, so you always know who's accountable for your production, operations, and technology. Headshots and full bios drop in as the roster is confirmed.

Who Runs What.

Level 01 · Command

Production

Executive Producer

Owns the production end to end: vision, budget, and the standard the whole build holds to.

Production

Senior Producer

Leads the production day to day: scope, schedule, vendors, and the build.

Production

Associate Producer

Level 02 · Direction

Production

Production Director

Runs the build: creative, scenic, staging, and technical production.

Operations

Operations Director

Runs the day-of: logistics, crew, safety, and compliance.

Hospitality

Hospitality Director

Owns guest experience, VIP, and hospitality operations.

Level 03 · Management

Project

Project Manager

Holds the plan: scope, schedule, deliverables, and owners.

Project

Project Coordinator

Keeps the plan moving: documents, comms, and tracking.

Logistics

Moves freight and crew: load-in and out, transport, and vendors.

Production
